# Missions

Missions are readable records for concrete adaptive loop runs. Use `mission-{number}-{slug}.md`.

The body for a newly framed mission contains Objective, Scope, Delivery, Operating Envelope, Roles, Current State, Gates, Validation, Stop, Next Actions, and Journal. Delivery records coherent high-level themes, dependencies, affected contract families, planning classifications, selected-loop capability evidence, one `spec-driven` or `adaptive` approach, the current theme, and its route without becoming an implementation plan. Historical missions without Delivery remain readable and need no unrelated migration. The mission is the readable orchestration map for the journey until conclusion. Keep all content before `## Journal` at or below 300 physical lines. The Journal heading and content are excluded from that quota and may grow as needed to preserve useful history; large evidence still belongs elsewhere.

Keep the mission title in frontmatter only; begin the body with `## Objective`. `loop` stores the one selected loop ID; add a one-line `Loop fit:` statement only when the choice is non-obvious or materially affects authority. Pipeline declarations are capability inventory rather than an automatic work queue, while the approved persisted delivery route is obligatory after mission execution approval. `plan-required` requires approved plan coverage but not a dedicated plan, `plan-recommended` makes a separate plan optional, and `direct-capable` needs no separate plan unless promoted. `spec-driven` requires at least one approved, validated plan whose combined coverage spans the complete confirmed mission scope before execution, with evidence-backed rationale for each additional plan; `adaptive` still plans `plan-required` themes and may advance other themes incrementally until promotion evidence appears. Operating Envelope records human intervention as `less`, `more`, or `context-dependent`, mapped respectively to the existing `delegated_until_escalation`, `user_mediated`, or `mixed` profile, together with delegated and user authority. Current State uses a non-terminal phase from the selected loop and names the active workflow, pending gate, resume trigger, and validation target. Mission `status` records lifecycle state, while a Journal gate receipt records execution approval. `pipeline-start-mission` records a `draft` and stops without invoking planning or execution. Use `pipeline-execute-mission` for readiness review of either a `draft` or `active` mission and explicit approval before its selected loop starts or resumes. An `active` status does not by itself prove execution approval; an active mission with a pending execution gate or a Journal statement that execution has not started proceeds to readiness instead of correction.

Statuses: `draft`, `active`, `blocked`, `done`. A done mission has no active gates or Next Actions; put durable follow-up in a new mission or an approved related outcome.
