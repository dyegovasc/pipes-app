# Fragment: Reflection

## Instructions

1. Compare the result with the objective and current strategy.
2. Identify what changed, what remains uncertain, and what failed.
3. Update assumptions from evidence rather than defending the prior approach.
4. Choose correction, further validation, a new strategy, durable recording, or a real stop condition.
5. Avoid creating journal noise for routine internal iteration.

## Output

- Updated understanding
- Progress and residual risk
- Strategy adjustment or next action
