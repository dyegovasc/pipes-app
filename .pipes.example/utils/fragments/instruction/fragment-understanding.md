# Fragment: Understanding

## Instructions

1. Explain what the observed evidence means for the objective.
2. Separate verified facts from assumptions and unresolved questions.
3. Identify affected boundaries, material risks, and contradictions.
4. Mark which unknowns are discoverable and which require a user choice.
5. Keep the analysis at the level needed to choose action; do not persist private chain-of-thought.

## Output

- Working understanding
- Assumptions and unknowns
- Risks and contradictions
- Decisions that need authority
