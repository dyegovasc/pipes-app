# Fragment: Strategy

## Instructions

1. Compare direct action, deterministic pipeline, further observation, validation, or a user question.
2. Prefer the route that produces useful evidence or progress with the least scope and ceremony.
3. Use a pipeline only when its known phase contract fits the need.
4. Change strategy when repeated work adds no progress or new evidence.
5. Keep protected actions and user-owned choices visible.

## Output

- Selected strategy and rationale
- Expected result and validation
- Candidate next actions
