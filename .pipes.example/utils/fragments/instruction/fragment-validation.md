# Fragment: Validation

## Instructions

1. Map each important success or acceptance criterion to concrete evidence.
2. Run the most relevant available tests, inspections, or real-surface checks.
3. Record failures, skipped checks, and limitations explicitly.
4. Distinguish a defect in the work from a defect in the validation setup.
5. Do not infer completion from confidence when direct evidence is available.

## Output

- Validation result
- Evidence and checks
- Failures or gaps
- Required correction or follow-up
